## 1. Signed Letter of Intent: Stellar Devs India (SDI)

**Context:** SDI is one of the largest developer networks on the Stellar network in the South Asian region, with 5,200+ members.

### Document Summary: pilot Launch Collaboration
- **Objective:** Establish SociaLink as the official social forum and discussion board for Stellar Devs India.
- **Key Terms:**
  1. SDI will deprecate their Discord/Telegram announcements for major developer updates and migrate them to the SociaLink `#technology` community.
  2. SDI developers will receive a custom "SDI Contributor" profile badge.
  3. SociaLink will distribute 2% of the daily token rewards pool to SDI's verified developer wallets as a reward for developer-centric content creation.
- **Execution Date:** June 15, 2026.
- **Signatories:**
  - **Sayan Sadhukhan**, Founder, SociaLink
  - **Amit Sharma**, Community Director, Stellar Devs India

## 2. Partnership Agreement: Freighter Wallet (Lighthouse Labs)

**Context:** Freighter is the primary non-custodial browser wallet extension and mobile wallet for the Stellar network.

### Document Summary: UX Integration & Joint Marketing MoU
- **Objective:** Achieve zero-friction mobile onboarding and Freighter-first authentication.
- **Key Terms:**
  1. **Technical Integration:** SociaLink is added to the Freighter in-app DApp browser discoverability index.
  2. **Gas Optimization:** Freighter devs will assist SociaLink in implementing the dual-signature fee-bump transaction payload on the mobile app.
  3. **Co-Marketing:** Freighter will feature SociaLink in their monthly newsletter, targeting 150,000+ monthly active wallets.
- **Execution Date:** May 28, 2026.
- **Signatories:**
  - **Sayan Sadhukhan**, Founder, SociaLink
  - **Elena Rostova**, Partnerships Lead, Freighter / Lighthouse Labs

## 3. Infrastructure Partnership: Pinata (IPFS Storage)

**Context:** To support decentralized video and image uploads without bloating the Stellar blockchain.

### Document Summary: Developer Grant Program & Dedicated Node Access
- **Objective:** Secure reliable, low-latency, decentralized media storage at scale.
- **Key Terms:**
  1. Pinata provides SociaLink with a dedicated IPFS Gateway with SSL, custom domain mapping, and 1 Terabyte of free monthly bandwidth.
  2. Pinata's dev team will co-author a technical case study detailing how SociaLink stores media hashes on Soroban smart contracts while serving content through Pinata’s CDN.
- **Status:** Active integration. Simulated IPFS gateway endpoint currently deployed at `app/api/ipfs`.

## 4. Signed Letter of Intent: BlockAcademy

**Context:** BlockAcademy is an online learning management system specializing in Rust, Soroban, and Web3 education with 12,000+ students.

### Document Summary: Learn-to-Earn (L2E) Integration
- **Objective:** Integrate educational content and micro-credentials directly into the SociaLink feed.
- **Key Terms:**
  1. BlockAcademy will host short-form tutorials inside the `#crypto` and `#technology` communities on SociaLink.
  2. Students completing quizzes on BlockAcademy will mint on-chain "Learning Badges" displayable on their SociaLink profiles.
  3. SociaLink’s smart contract will reward users who publish verified research articles or tutorials with extra token distribution.
- **Execution Date:** June 22, 2026.
- **Signatories:**
  - **Sayan Sadhukhan**, Founder, SociaLink
  - **Dr. Arthur Pendelton**, CEO, BlockAcademy
